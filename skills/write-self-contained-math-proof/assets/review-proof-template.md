## Abstract

In one self-contained paragraph, identify the problem and setting, state the
main result at its exact scope, name the central proof mechanism, and record the

## 1. Introduction

Explain the mathematical setting and why the result matters to the intended
reader. State the main theorem with all hypotheses and quantifiers. Describe the
central idea of the proof, including the decisive intermediate steps, and end
with a short guide to the remaining sections.

### Main theorem

State the exact theorem in complete mathematical sentences, using formulas
for its actual maps, bounds and identities. Prefer concise standard terms to
new abbreviations. Define genuinely necessary nonstandard notation immediately
before the statement or in a short opening conventions paragraph.

## 2. Preliminaries

### 2.1. Conventions and notation

Fix ambient categories, base objects, indexing conventions, and every symbol

is possible.

### 2.2. Definitions and external inputs

Give the definitions needed by the proof. State each cited theorem in the exact
form used, record its source locator, and match its hypotheses to the present
setting. Cite the source of a borrowed definition at its introduction. Original
auxiliary proofs also belong here when they supply the interfaces for the main
argument. Add or remove subsections according to the mathematics.
